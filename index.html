The Gate Endures

Field manual

Hold the Gate

Twenty waves. One covenant. No second sanctuary.

The siege

Enemies follow the pilgrim road to the Gate. Each one that reaches it breaks a seal. At zero seals, the sanctuary falls.

Guardians

Choose a relic in the Armory, then click a glowing rune pad. Guardians fire on their own. Click a built guardian to upgrade or sell it.

Sanctum

Build Manna Wells, Reliquaries, and Oracle Tents in the four sanctum courts near the Gate. They pay gold at the end of every wave.

The Bearer

Move with WASD or click the ground. The Bearer attacks nearby foes automatically. Level up to choose roguelite blessings.
